# Concept

## Central idea

The video does not illustrate kindness with sentimental imagery. It reveals a recurring mechanism.

Two agents are represented as points. Kindness is represented as the maintenance, repair, or strengthening of the connection between them.

The same underlying visual primitive — a thread — changes form throughout the film:

- help becomes a shared path;
- sharing becomes redistribution that creates relation;
- comfort becomes co-regulation;
- truth becomes a difficult signal that preserves a deeper channel;
- admitting error becomes model correction;
- forgiveness becomes reconnection with a visible scar;
- trust becomes greater capacity in an edge;
- community becomes a resilient network.

The viewer should gradually discover that apparently different behaviours share a structural family resemblance.

## Governing rule

For every visual idea, ask:

> Does this show what kindness **does**, rather than merely illustrate the noun?

If not, remove it.
